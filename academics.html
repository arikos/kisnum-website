Academic Programs & Curriculum

Fully accredited 2.5-year clinical certificates structured in accordance with national health education regulations and international patient care benchmarks.

2.5 YEARS DURATION ACCREDITED

Certificate in General Nursing

Prepares students to deliver high-quality bedside nursing, administer medical interventions, manage surgical wards, and provide compassionate healthcare in diverse hospital environments.

Core Curriculum Modules:

  • Human Anatomy, Physiology & Biochemistry
  • Fundamental Nursing Principles & Bedside Care
  • Clinical Pharmacology & Medication Safety
  • Medical & Surgical Nursing Practicals
  • First Aid, Emergency & Trauma Management
  • Public Health, Hygiene & Community Nursing
  • Medical Ethics, Professionalism & Legal Practice
  • German / French Healthcare Language Track

Certificate in Midwifery

Specialized education focused on maternal health, prenatal clinical monitoring, safe childbirth management, postnatal care, and newborn resuscitation.

Core Curriculum Modules:

  • Reproductive Anatomy & Female Physiology
  • Antenatal Screening, Care & Maternal Nutrition
  • Labor Management & Safe Delivery Practicals
  • Emergency Obstetric & Neonatal Resuscitation
  • Postnatal Care & Family Health Leadership
  • Gynaecological & Child Health Nursing
  • Community Reproductive Health Mobilization
  • Digital Health Records & E-Health Literacy

Learning Methodology

Hands-On Practical Clinical Mastery

We balance classroom instruction with intensive clinical skill simulations and direct hospital rotations.

Simulation Skill Labs

Students practice clinical maneuvers, IV insertion, catheterization, and delivery simulations on high-fidelity manikins before touching patients.

Hospital Rotations

Supervised ward placements in regional referral hospitals ensure real-world patient care experience under certified clinical instructors.

Digital Health Training

Training in modern digital health technologies, patient record management, and ICT laboratory modules alongside medical studies.
