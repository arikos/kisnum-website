About Our Institution

Dedicated to practical clinical excellence, integrity, and world-class health education.

WHO WE ARE

Nurturing Compassionate Health Professionals

Kituuza School of Nursing & Midwifery is a center of excellence established to train the next generation of healthcare professionals in Uganda and beyond.

Located along Katosi Road in Kisoga, Mukono, our modern campus combines hands-on practical clinical simulation labs with direct hospital ward rotations. We equip candidates with the practical maneuvers, ethical discipline, and emergency care response skills required in modern healthcare environments.

OUR GUIDING PRINCIPLES

Vision, Mission & Core Values

Our Vision

To be a leading healthcare training institution recognized nationally and internationally for practical clinical precision and ethical care.

Our Mission

To empower competent, compassionate nurses and midwives through rigorous academic instruction, clinical simulations, and regional ward practice.

Core Values

  • Clinical Integrity & Precision
  • Compassionate Patient Care
  • Disciplined Professionalism
  • Global Language Integration
