Director's Message

Tempered in Duty, Dedicated to Global Health Excellence

"In the heat of battle medical operations, there is no second guessing. Precision, split-second discipline, deep empathy, and absolute composure are what separate survival from loss. That is the exact DNA we instill in every student at Kituuza."

My medical career was forged under the most demanding conditions imaginable. Serving as a medical officer alongside the Uganda People's Defence Forces (UPDF) took me directly into intense conflict zones across Northern Uganda during the LRA insurgency, the arid battlegrounds of Sudan, and high-stakes peacekeeping deployments under AMISOM in Somalia.

Treating severe trauma under fire taught me priceless lessons about human resilience, time consciousness, unwavering integrity, and the sacred value of medical duty. In combat medicine, excuses do not exist. Success requires emotional strength, flawless technical execution, and immediate cohesion under pressure.

It is precisely this military-grade discipline and operational rigor that I brought into founding Kituuza School of Nursing & Midwifery. I made a firm promise: our institution will never produce passive academic certificate holders. Instead, we instill our learners with absolute punctuality, clinical sharpness, ethical firmness, and an unshakable commitment to patient care.

Furthermore, recognizing that modern healthcare is a global market, I introduced two unique foundational pillars into our core curriculum:

Foreign Language Tracks

I envisioned nurses who can seamlessly step into European and international health systems. We integrate mandatory German (B1/B2) and French language modules right alongside clinical rotations.

Music & Creative Discipline

Medicine demands mental balance and teamwork. Through our institutional band and music programs, we build emotional resilience, rhythm, precision, and student camaraderie.

When you send your child or step into Kituuza yourself, you are joining an environment where character is built with intent. We prepare you not just to work in a local ward, but to stand out with distinction anywhere in the world.

Dr. James Kiyengo Director, Kituuza School of Nursing & Midwifery
